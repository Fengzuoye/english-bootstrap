## 1. 基础概念

"sentence" is a noun.
The meaning of "sentence" is "a group of words that says something".
Example: "This is a sentence."

"form" is a noun.
The meaning of "form" is "one way that a word is; go, goes, and went are the same word in different ways".
Example: "Went is a form of go."

## 2. 词性

"noun" is a noun.
The meaning of "noun" is "a word for a person, a thing, or a place".
Example: "Water is a noun."

"verb" is a noun.
The meaning of "verb" is "a word that says what someone or something does, or what happens".
Example: "Eat is a verb."

"adjective" is a noun.
The meaning of "adjective" is "a word that says what a person or thing is like".
Example: "Good is an adjective."

"adverb" is a noun.
The meaning of "adverb" is "a word that says how, when, or where something happens".
Example: "Here is an adverb."

"preposition" is a noun.
The meaning of "preposition" is "a word like in, on, at, to, from, of, for, with, by, about, as, like, after, before, under, over, or between".
Example: "In is a preposition."

"conjunction" is a noun.
The meaning of "conjunction" is "a word like and, or, but, if, because, so, when, where, why, how, whose, or than; it puts two parts of a sentence in one sentence".
Example: "And is a conjunction."

"pronoun" is a noun.
The meaning of "pronoun" is "a word like I, you, he, she, it, we, they, who, what, which, someone, something, this, that, these, those, one, other, or same; we use it for a person or thing that we said before, or for a person or thing that we know".
Example: "He is a pronoun."

"determiner" is a noun.
The meaning of "determiner" is "a word like all, some, any, each, every, many, much, more, most, few, little, or less; we use it before a noun to say how many or which one".
Example: "All is a determiner."

"article" is a noun.
The meaning of "article" is "a word like a, an, or the; we put it before a noun".
Example: "The is an article."

"interjection" is a noun.
The meaning of "interjection" is "a word like oh, please, sorry, thanks, hello, or goodbye; we say it by itself to say a feeling".
Example: "Oh is an interjection."

"modal" is a noun.
The meaning of "modal" is "a word like can, could, may, might, must, will, would, or should; we use it with other verbs to say what can happen, must happen, or may happen".
Example: "Can is a modal."

"auxiliary" is a noun.
The meaning of "auxiliary" is "a small verb like be, have, or do; we use it with other verbs to make forms".
Example: "Do is an auxiliary."

## 3. 句子结构

"subject" is a noun.
The meaning of "subject" is "the person or thing that does what the verb says".
Example: "In 'He sees the sun', 'he' is the subject."

"object" is a noun.
The meaning of "object" is "the person or thing that something is done to".
Example: "In 'He sees the sun', 'the sun' is the object."

"phrase" is a noun.
The meaning of "phrase" is "a group of words that is part of a sentence".
Example: "The words 'in the water' are a phrase."

"clause" is a noun.
The meaning of "clause" is "a group of words with a subject and a verb; a sentence can have one of them or more than one of them".
Example: "In 'He comes and I go', there are two clauses."

## 4. 名词的数与冠词

"singular" is a noun.
The meaning of "singular" is "one; the form of a noun for one person or thing".
Example: "'Day' is singular."

"plural" is a noun.
The meaning of "plural" is "more than one; the form of a noun for more than one person or thing".
Example: "'Days' is the plural of 'day'."

"countable" is an adjective.
The meaning of "countable" is "of a noun: we can put a number before it, like one day or two days".
Example: "Day is a countable noun."

"uncountable" is an adjective.
The meaning of "uncountable" is "of a noun: we cannot put a number before it, like water".
Example: "Water is an uncountable noun."

"definite" is an adjective.
The meaning of "definite" is "of the article 'the': we use it for a person or thing that we know, or for the only one".
Example: "'The' is the definite article."

"indefinite" is an adjective.
The meaning of "indefinite" is "of the articles 'a' and 'an': we use them for one person or thing, not one that we know".
Example: "'A' is the indefinite article."

"possessive" is an adjective.
The meaning of "possessive" is "of a word or form: it says that something is of a person or thing".
Example: "'My' is a possessive word."

"positive" is an adjective.
The meaning of "positive" is "of a sentence: it has no 'not'; it says that something is true".
Example: "'He is old' is positive."

We put 's' after most nouns to make the plural: day → days, hand → hands.
Some nouns are different in other ways: man → men, woman → women, child → children, person → people.
We put 's after a noun to make the possessive: the man's hand.
We use 'some' with uncountable nouns and with plural nouns: some water, some days.
We use 'a' or 'an' with a singular countable noun: a day, an eye.
We use 'the' with a person or thing that we know: the sun.
We use 'many' with plural nouns and 'much' with uncountable nouns: many days, much water.
We use 'few' with plural nouns and 'little' with uncountable nouns: few days, little water.
## 5. 代词

"reflexive" is an adjective.
The meaning of "reflexive" is "of a word like myself, yourself, himself, herself, itself, ourselves, or themselves: it says that the person or thing does something to itself".
Example: "In 'I see myself', 'myself' is reflexive."

"each other" is a pronoun.
The meaning of "each other" is "a group of words that we use for two or more people or things when each one does the same thing to the other ones".
Example: "We see each other every day."

"everyone" is a pronoun.
The meaning of "everyone" is "all people; every person".
Example: "Everyone is here."

"everything" is a pronoun.
The meaning of "everything" is "all things".
Example: "Everything is here."

"nothing" is a pronoun.
The meaning of "nothing" is "no thing; not any thing".
Example: "I see nothing."

"anyone" is a pronoun.
The meaning of "anyone" is "any person".
Example: "Is anyone here?"

"anything" is a pronoun.
The meaning of "anything" is "any thing".
Example: "Do you see anything?"

"nobody" is a pronoun.
The meaning of "nobody" is "no person".
Example: "Nobody is here."

"somebody" is a pronoun.
The meaning of "somebody" is "someone".
Example: "Somebody is here."

Pronouns have subject forms and object forms: I see him; he sees me.
This and these are for things with me; that and those are for things not with me.
We use 'each other' for two people or things when each one does the same thing to the other one: We see each other.

## 6. 时间、频率与序数词

"today" is a noun.
The meaning of "today" is "the day that is now".
Example: "We eat today."

"yesterday" is a noun.
The meaning of "yesterday" is "the day before today".
Example: "I saw the sun yesterday."

"tomorrow" is a noun.
The meaning of "tomorrow" is "the day after today".
Example: "We will eat tomorrow."

"always" is an adverb.
The meaning of "always" is "at all times".
Example: "He always works."

"never" is an adverb.
The meaning of "never" is "not at any time".
Example: "I never sleep in the day."

"often" is an adverb.
The meaning of "often" is "many times".
Example: "He often works at night."

"sometimes" is an adverb.
The meaning of "sometimes" is "some times".
Example: "I sometimes see the moon at night."

"usually" is an adverb.
The meaning of "usually" is "most times".
Example: "He usually works in the day."

"first" is an adjective.
The meaning of "first" is "the one before all other ones".
Example: "The first day of the year is today."

"second" is an adjective.
The meaning of "second" is "the one after first".
Example: "The second day is tomorrow."

"third" is an adjective.
The meaning of "third" is "the one after second".
Example: "The third day is a new day."

## 7. 时态起步

"tense" is a noun.
The meaning of "tense" is "the form of a verb that tells when something happens or is true".
Example: "Tense tells when."

"present" is a noun.
The meaning of "present" is "the time now".
Example: "The present is now."

"past" is a noun.
The meaning of "past" is "the time before now".
Example: "The past is before now."

"future" is a noun.
The meaning of "future" is "the time after now".
Example: "The future is after now."

"simple" is an adjective.
The meaning of "simple" is "of a tense: for true things and for things that happen many times".
Example: "Simple forms have no 'ing'."

"action" is a noun.
The meaning of "action" is "something that someone or something does".
Example: "Eating is an action."

"present participle" is a noun.
The meaning of "present participle" is "the form of a verb with 'ing', like 'eating'; we use it with be".
Example: "Eating is a present participle."

"past participle" is a noun.
The meaning of "past participle" is "the form of a verb that we use with have and be; for most verbs it has 'ed', like 'worked'".
Example: "Worked is a past participle."

"present simple" is a noun.
The meaning of "present simple" is "the tense for true things and for things that happen many times; for he, she, and it, we put 's' after the verb".
Example: "He works every day."

"present continuous" is a noun.
The meaning of "present continuous" is "the tense for actions that are going on now; we make it with be and the present participle".
Example: "I am eating now."

"past simple" is a noun.
The meaning of "past simple" is "the tense for things that happened at a past time; for most verbs we put 'ed' after the verb".
Example: "I ate food yesterday."

"past continuous" is a noun.
The meaning of "past continuous" is "the tense for actions that were going on at a past time; we make it with was or were and the present participle".
Example: "I was eating when he came."

"present perfect" is a noun.
The meaning of "present perfect" is "the tense for things that happened before now and are true now; we make it with have or has and the past participle".
Example: "I have eaten."

"future simple" is a noun.
The meaning of "future simple" is "the tense for things that will happen after now; we make it with will and the verb".
Example: "I will eat tomorrow."

We use the present simple for true things and for things that happen many times: He works every day.
We use the present continuous for actions that are going on now: I am eating now.
We use the past simple for things that happened at a past time: I ate food yesterday.
We use the present perfect for things that happened before now and are true now: I have eaten food, so I do not need more food.
We use 'will' with the verb for the future: I will eat tomorrow.
We can use the present continuous for a future time that we know: I am eating tomorrow.

## 8. 疑问、否定与词序

"question" is a noun.
The meaning of "question" is "a sentence that asks something".
Example: "'What is this?' is a question."

"negative" is a noun.
The meaning of "negative" is "a sentence or word that says no or not".
Example: "'I am not old' is a negative sentence."

"statement" is a noun.
The meaning of "statement" is "a sentence that says something; it is not a question".
Example: "'He is old' is a statement."

We use 'some' in positive sentences and 'any' in questions and negatives: I have some water; do you have any water?
In a statement, the subject comes before the verb: He sees the sun.
In a question, the auxiliary or modal comes before the subject: Are you here? Can he see me?
We make questions with do, does, or did for other verbs: Do you see the sun? Did he eat?
We put 'not' after be, have, or a modal: I am not old; he is not here.
For other verbs, we use do not or does not: I do not see the moon.
We put adverbs like always, never, often, sometimes, and usually before the verb: He always works.
We put these adverbs after be: He is never old.
