# 第 3 层：牛津 3000 · 第 1 批（高频日常词）

> 从本层开始逐批定义 Oxford 3000。每批一个新层文件（layer3.md、layer4.md…），
> 全部只用“第 0 层 + 前面各层已定义词”。本批 25 词按依赖顺序排列：
> 先学会 learn / read，才能定义 book / school；先学会 city，才能定义 town。

## 学习与家庭

"learn" is a verb.
The meaning of "learn" is "come to know".
Example: "I learn new words every day."

"read" is a verb.
The meaning of "read" is "see words and know what they say".
Example: "I read words every day."

"book" is a noun.
The meaning of "book" is "a thing with words that people read".
Example: "I read a book."

"house" is a noun.
The meaning of "house" is "a place where people live".
Example: "I live in a house."

"home" is a noun.
The meaning of "home" is "the place where a person lives".
Example: "I am at home now."

"school" is a noun.
The meaning of "school" is "a place where children go to learn".
Example: "Children go to school in the morning."

"teacher" is a noun.
The meaning of "teacher" is "a person who makes other people learn".
Example: "The teacher is at school."

"student" is a noun.
The meaning of "student" is "a person who learns".
Example: "The student reads a book."

"story" is a noun.
The meaning of "story" is "words that say what happened to a person or thing".
Example: "He tells a story about the old man."

"answer" is a noun.
The meaning of "answer" is "the words that you say when someone asks you something".
Example: "He gives the answer to my question."

"mother" is a noun.
The meaning of "mother" is "the woman who has a child".
Example: "My mother works at home."

"father" is a noun.
The meaning of "father" is "the man who has a child".
Example: "My father works in the day."

"parent" is a noun.
The meaning of "parent" is "a mother or a father".
Example: "My parents work at night."

## 时间与地方

"week" is a noun.
The meaning of "week" is "seven days".
Example: "There are seven days in a week."

"city" is a noun.
The meaning of "city" is "a big place where many people live".
Example: "Many people live in the city."

"town" is a noun.
The meaning of "town" is "a place where many people live; it is smaller than a city".
Example: "The town is smaller than the city."

"village" is a noun.
The meaning of "village" is "a small town".
Example: "The village is near the town."

## 性质与动作

"tall" is an adjective.
The meaning of "tall" is "high".
Example: "The man is tall."

"warm" is an adjective.
The meaning of "warm" is "a little hot".
Example: "The day is warm."

"cool" is an adjective.
The meaning of "cool" is "a little cold".
Example: "The night is cool."

"easy" is an adjective.
The meaning of "easy" is "not hard".
Example: "This work is easy."

"difficult" is an adjective.
The meaning of "difficult" is "not easy".
Example: "That work is difficult."

"begin" is a verb.
The meaning of "begin" is "start".
Example: "The day begins with the morning."

"end" is a verb.
The meaning of "end" is "stop".
Example: "The day ends at night."

"price" is a noun.
The meaning of "price" is "the money that you give for something".
Example: "The price of food is high."
